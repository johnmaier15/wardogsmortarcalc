        How to use
        
    
          
  1. In game, open the tactical map and hover the cursor over your mortar so the X / Y readout shows. Take a screenshot.
    2. 
          
  2. Paste it into slot 1. The app reads the numbers. If it misreads, drag a box tightly around the coordinate text, or just type the values.
    3. 
          
  3. Do the same for the target in slot 2. You can also paste a squad-chat line from Mark Coordinates.
    4. 
          
  4. In the sight, turn until the compass strip reads the azimuth, then dial RNG to the range shown. The sight's MILS readout should land near the elevation figure; RNG is the one to trust. Fire, then use the miss adjuster for the next round.
    5. 
        

        
Coordinates: 1.00 = 100 m, so 0.01 = 1 m. Azimuth: 0° north, clockwise. Range is straight geometry from the two points. The elevation-in-mils figures are community measurements from the MIT-licensed wardogs-calculator project and may drift between patches. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with BULKHEAD.
